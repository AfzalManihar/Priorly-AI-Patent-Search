Questions

What does Priorly search?

A corpus of granted patents and published applications. The demo uses a fictional set so you can explore the interface safely.

How is relevance measured?

Against the references that examiners cited on granted patents. Recall@10 and precision@10 are reported after every pipeline stage.

Why split a claim into elements?

One prior-art document rarely covers a whole claim. Searching elements separately finds the pieces, and the coverage view shows how they combine.

Is this legal advice?

No. Priorly is a research tool. A patent professional should review any conclusion about validity.
